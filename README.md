## 📖 About the Project

**Marvel Universe: A Journey Through Time** is a cinematic, editorial interactive chronological exhibition tracking the full timeline of the Marvel Cinematic Universe (Earth-616) and Multiverse—spanning from the primordial **Big Bang (~13.8 Billion BC)** through the monumental eras of **Avengers: Endgame**, the **Multiverse Saga**, and looking ahead to **Avengers: Doomsday (2026+ AD)**.

Designed with a **space-elevator narrative architecture**, the exhibition takes visitors on an archival descent through history, featuring 36 curated landmark events, each illustrated with verified cinematic artwork.

---

## ✨ Features

- ⏱️ **36 Verified Chronological Milestones:** From the creation of the Celestials and the Vibranium meteorite impact in Wakanda, to Iron Man's sacrifice and Doctor Doom's impending arrival.
- 🎨 **Cinematic Dark & Crimson Design System:** Tailored with official Marvel comic collage aesthetics, warm dark-crimson glassmorphism, and elegant gold accents.
- ⚡ **Pure HTML5 + CSS3:** Built with zero runtime JavaScript and zero third-party UI frameworks for maximum speed, accessibility, and smooth 60fps scrolling.
- 📱 **Fully Responsive:** Fluid layouts designed for smartphones, tablets, laptops, and ultra-wide displays.
- 🔤 **Curated Typography:** Powered by Google Fonts (*Cinzel* for monumental headings, *Space Grotesk* for editorial reading, and *Space Mono* for telemetry coordinates).

## 🗺️ Timeline Phases Covered

1. **Phase 1: Cosmic Origins** (~13.8 Billion BC – 2988 BC)
2. **Phase 2: Ancient Earth & Mystic Foundations** (1000 AD – 1845 AD)
3. **Phase 3: Colonial & World War II Era** (1942 AD – 1946 AD)
4. **Phase 4: Cold War & Pre-Modern Era** (1951 AD – 1988 AD)
5. **Phase 5: Gamma Bio-Tech & The Thaw** (2005 AD – 2011 AD)
6. **Phase 6: Age of Heroes & Cosmic Convergence** (2012 AD – 2014 AD)
7. **Phase 7: Fall of S.H.I.E.L.D. & Rise of Ultron** (2014 AD – 2016 AD)
8. **Phase 8: Infinity War & The Time Heist** (2018 AD – 2023 AD)
9. **Phase 9: Multiverse Incursions & Beyond** (2024 AD – 2026+ AD)

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

1. **Clone the repository:**
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/ShahZmn/workshop.git
   cd workshop
   ```

2. **Open in browser:**
   - Double-click `index.html` to open it in your default web browser, or:
   - Run a simple local server:
     ```bash
     python -m http.server 3000
     ```
     and navigate to `http://localhost:3000`.
